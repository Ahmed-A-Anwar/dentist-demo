What we do

Cleaning

Scale, polish and floss to clear tartar and surface stains. Most people need one every six months. It takes about 45 minutes.

Whitening

A peroxide gel under a LED light lifts several shades in a single visit, and the tray we send home keeps it there.

Braces

Metal, ceramic or clear aligners for teenagers and adults. You get a scan, a plan and the full price before day one.

Implants

A titanium root topped with a crown that matches the tooth beside it. One visit to place it, three to four months to heal.

One dentist, start to finish.

Bright Smile Dental opened on Road 9 in Maadi in 2014. You see the same dentist at every visit, from the first check-up to the last retainer.

One patient sits in the chair at a time, and we go over every option and its cost before we begin. No treatment starts on the day you are asked.
